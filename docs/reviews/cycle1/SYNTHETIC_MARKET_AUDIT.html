01 — Verdict

Harden the instrument. Do not rebuild the market, and do not start the mechanistic track.

You asked whether to restructure the simulator or harden it. Neither framing quite fits, because the environment is not where the deepest problem sits. The generator has real defects and I list nine of them. But the load-bearing failure is one layer up, in the agent–environment interface, and it is invisible in synthetic_market.py.

Three things are true at once, and they set the whole plan:

  1. The hidden fundamental value is not hidden. Vt is recoverable exactly from two fields the agent is shown. The decoupling of price from value — the property the entire benchmark rests on — is not enforced in the observation space.
  2. Context does not accumulate. Every decision is a fresh two-message prompt. No history is ever appended. There is no growing context for a mandate to decay against, so Mandate Salience Decay as defined cannot be the mechanism producing the results.
  3. The headline temporal result is reproduced by a null model with no decay in it. Two constant-exposure arms that never change behaviour already produce a 3.63× Q1→Q4 gap growth, against the paper's 4.4×, because the statistic is a running minimum.

Given your reframing — the market is the measurement instrument, not the subject; no claim of real-trading applicability — this is unambiguously good news. Every defect above is a measurement defect, and measurement defects are cheap to fix relative to realism defects. Nothing here requires an order book, multiple assets, or other market participants. The revision plan's “Port to a ground-truth-preserving simulator (ABIDES)” item should be dropped: it spends the entire six weeks buying realism the reframing has already told us we do not need, and it would not fix a single one of the three failures above.

The recommendation

Keep the generator's architecture. Fix what it emits, fix how the agent consumes it, and fix what the metrics score. Then make context accumulation the paper's independent variable rather than an assumption. Concretely: the existing 18-model grid is not garbage — it is mislabelled. It is a clean memoryless control. Run a second arm in which context genuinely accumulates, and MSD becomes the difference between the two arms rather than a property asserted about one of them. That is a stronger identification strategy than anything currently in the paper, it directly answers reviewer VG7b's confound at the level of the independent variable, and it costs one scoped sub-grid instead of a full re-run.

Against the timeline: the mechanistic track (M1–M4) must be deferred in full. M1 as specified — logging attention mass on the mandate span across the 200-day rollout — has no measurand in the current code, because the prompt is byte-identical in structure at step 1 and step 200. Building a mechanistic section on top of an instrument that does not yet measure its construct moves the same criticism up one level, where it will be harder to answer. Fix the instrument for ICLR; the mechanism is the natural follow-up paper and it will be much easier to write once the interface actually accumulates context.

Where the paper and the code disagree

Each item below was verified against the running code, not read off the source. Evidence lines are output from probe scripts over the actual generator at the paper's five seeds and T=200.

05 — Hostile read

What nobody has raised yet

These are the findings that matter for the reframing, because each one attacks the claim that the environment yields an objective ground truth. All are empirically verified against the shipped generator.

F-1The hidden fundamental value is exactly recoverable from the observation spaceBlocking

Earnings are defined as EPS = V/15 and the reported multiple as P/E = P/EPS. Therefore P/E = 15·P/V, and the agent — which is shown both price and reported_PE — can invert to V = 15·P / (P/E).

Evidence
V reconstructed as 15*price/reported_PE, bull_trap seed 42, T=200
max absolute error over 200 days : 0.0000000000
sign(P-V) == sign(PE-15) agreement : 100.00% of steps

Three consequences, in increasing order of severity. First, Table 2's “Hidden from the Agent” and get_observation's “Masking Fundamental Value” are both false. Second, the Rationality Gap's entire ground-truth criterion, sign(P−V), is a threshold on a single displayed field: reported_PE > 15. Third and worst, 15 is the canonical textbook fair-value multiple. A model carrying the ordinary real-world prior that a P/E near 15 is fairly valued will score well on RG for reasons that have nothing to do with the synthetic environment — which reintroduces exactly the memorised-prior contamination the synthetic market was built to eliminate. The paper's central methodological claim is refuted by its own observation space.

F-2Where the ground truth is hidden, it is not resolvableBlocking

RG scores each action against sign(Pt−Vt) with no dead zone. Whenever |P−V| falls inside the generator's own noise, the “objectively correct action” is a coin flip driven by a draw the agent cannot observe.

Evidence
fraction of steps with |P-V|/V < 1%, mean over 5 seeds
flat : 96.9% (mean |P-V|/V = 0.35%)
bull_trap : 28.1% (phase 1 alone: 66.2%)
crash : 0.0% (constant 8% discount)

So the instrument has a well-posed ground truth on roughly 60% of bull-trap steps and 100% of crash steps, and an ill-posed one everywhere else. In the flat scenario the ground truth is essentially pure noise on 97% of steps. This is the single most important finding for your reframing: “we know the objectively correct action at every step” is not currently true, and it is the sentence the whole paper now rests on.

F-3The Rationality Gap rewards buying the bubble and holding it to the topBlocking

The HOLD clause is (Pt > Vt) ∨ (Ht > 0). Once an agent holds any stock, every subsequent HOLD is scored rational regardless of the mispricing. I ran the trivial non-learning policies against the metric.

Evidence
RG (%), mean over 5 seeds, T=200 bull_trap crash flat always HOLD (never buys) 79.8 0.0 50.6 BUY day 1, then HOLD forever 99.7 100.0 99.7 always SELL 79.8 0.0 50.6 random (1/3 each) 66.9 64.4 67.4 paper, bull_trap: static 85.9, memory 78.4

Read that table against the paper's headline. In the scenario designed to detect “ignoring fundamental value during speculative bubbles”, the policy that buys the bubble on day one and rides it to the peak scores 99.7% — a near-perfect rationality score for the exact failure the scenario exists to catch. Meanwhile a do-nothing agent scores 79.8%, which brackets both of our reported arms: static 85.9 sits 6 points above the do-nothing floor, and memory 78.4 sits below it. The 8.8% “rationality cost of re-grounding” that we report as a headline finding lives entirely inside the band between two policies that do no reasoning at all. The metric is dominated by a single binary — does the agent hold stock — and no baseline was ever reported that would have shown this.

F-4The Caricature Index has the wrong sign for panic sellingBlocking

CI is maximum drawdown on portfolio value, chosen because it is “highly sensitive to sustained panic-driven selling.” In a monotonically falling market, selling earlier reduces drawdown.

Evidence
crash scenario, seed 42, scripted policies buy & hold, never sells MDD = -50.27% panics & sells at day 100 MDD = -30.35% panics & sells at day 60 MDD = -15.26% 100% cash throughout MDD = 0.00% paper: static -26.18, memory -22.89, read as "static agents amplify panic-driven selling"

The ordering is monotone in the wrong direction: more panic selling → smaller drawdown. What MDD actually measures here is average equity exposure to a declining asset, which is largely set by Cideal. So the paper's crash interpretation — memory agents have a smaller drawdown, therefore re-grounding prevents panic selling — is backwards as a matter of construct validity. The same numbers are equally consistent with memory agents selling more. This is also the metric behind the 4.4× headline.

F-5MAS has a persona-asymmetric floor that no agent action can removeMaterial

MAS is |Ct − Cideal| where Ct is cash over mark-to-market portfolio value. A perfectly compliant agent that hits its target once and then never trades still accumulates MAS purely from price motion.

Evidence
passive MAS floor: buy once to hit Cideal, then never trade Cideal=0.2 Cideal=0.5 Cideal=1.0 flat 0.0233 0.0384 0.0000 bull_trap 0.0504 0.0924 0.0000 crash 0.0466 0.0638 0.0000 paper, flat: static 0.391 vs memory 0.342 (gap 0.049)

The floor is the same order of magnitude as the entire headline effect, and — critically — it is exactly zero for ISFJ and non-zero for ENTJ and INTJ. ISFJ's target is satisfiable by pure inaction; ENTJ's requires continuous rebalancing to hold a market-value ratio. That asymmetry runs in precisely the direction of the paper's flagship persona finding. Which raises the alternative explanation nobody has tested: any intervention that increases passivity will look like “helps ISFJ, hurts ENTJ”. The placebo does not rule this out, because the placebo does not change passivity. A single-mechanism account of our central bidirectional result is live and unaddressed.

F-6Context never accumulates, so MSD as defined cannot be what is being measuredBlocking

MSD is defined as mandate influence eroding “as market context accumulates over long horizons.” In StaticAgent.decide and ActiveMemoryAgent.decide, each step builds a fresh ChatPromptTemplate with exactly two messages — system persona, and today's observation — and calls chain.invoke. No history is appended anywhere. reset() is a no-op. Grep confirms no MessagesPlaceholder, no history buffer, anywhere in agent/.

The only state crossing the step boundary is two floats (cash and holdings) plus the market's own time-dependence. Prompt length at step 200 equals prompt length at step 1. Whatever produces the observed temporal patterns, it is not context accumulation, because there is none.

This has a direct bearing on the revision plan. M1 — “log attention mass on the mandate-token span across the 200-day rollout” — has no measurand. There is one prompt of fixed structure, repeated 200 times with different numbers in it. Any attention decay measurable in that setup is decay within a single short prompt, not the longitudinal phenomenon the paper names. M2 and M3 are unaffected in principle but lose their motivation. This needs to be settled before any GPU time is committed.

F-7The 4.4× compounding result is reproduced by a null model with no decayBlocking

The crash panel of Figure 3 plots CI_rolling = Drawdown_t.expanding().min() — a running minimum over an expanding window. A running minimum descends further the longer you watch it, so the gap between two running minima widens over time even when the underlying behaviours are constant. I simulated two arms with fixed, time-invariant equity exposure and no behavioural change of any kind.

Evidence
two CONSTANT-exposure arms (0.72 vs 0.62), no decay whatsoever median Q1->Q4 growth of the gap : 3.63x interquartile range : 3.34x - 3.69x paper: 4.4x, described as evidence that "a fixed difference in risk tolerance would instead produce a constant offset"

The sentence quoted above is the paper's explicit defense against the artifact reading, and it is false for this statistic. A null with zero decay delivers 3.63× against our reported 4.4×. The abstract, the introduction, contribution 3, and §4.3.2 all rest on this number. It must be recomputed with a non-cumulative statistic and re-tested against this null before it appears in another submission.

F-8Implied volatility and volume are deterministic clocks, not signalsMaterial

In bull_trap and crash, vol_regime and the volume multiplier are np.linspace ramps over phase indices, independent of any random draw.

Evidence
implied_volatility identical across all 5 seeds? bull_trap : True (range 15.0-37.5%) crash : True (range 15.0-45.0%)

So a field presented as a “forward-looking risk proxy” is a strictly monotone function of the day index, carrying no information except elapsed time — and the agent is separately given the day number in the date field. It is also not derived from the actual volatility of the generated price path; the 15×φ scaling is decorative. A reviewer who checks this will say the risk signal shown to the agent is not the risk of the process, and they will be right.

F-9Sentiment is i.i.d. and one of its three derived fields is a near-duplicateMinor
Evidence
flat scenario, T=200 lag-1 autocorrelation of news_sentiment : -0.008 corr(sentiment_change, news_sentiment) : +0.904

Because sentiment is drawn independently each step, sentiment_MA5 and sentiment_change carry almost no information beyond news_sentiment itself — sentiment_change correlates 0.90 with the raw series by construction. This is VG7b's W6 with a number attached. It is cheap to fix (an AR(1) with ρ≈0.7) and the fix costs nothing in ground-truth validity, so there is no reason to keep deflecting it.

F-10The advertised structural invariants never bindMinor

Appendix A says mania prices are floored at the phase-1 exit price and the blow-off at 1.05× the mania entry, “to guarantee bubble validity across all random seeds.” With a mania drift of +1.5/day over 60 days the blow-off floor sits near 106 while the path is near 190; likewise the crash's 0.98·V ceiling requires a noise draw above 0.06·V ≈ 6 against σ=1.5. Both are inert at every tested seed. Harmless in effect, but the appendix currently claims protection the code does not provide, and the claim is easy to check.

F-11Latent harness risks that have not yet caused damageClear, for now

Two failure modes exist in the loop that would be serious if they fired at scale. (a) After three failed attempts, decide() returns a synthetic HOLD with rationale "Error after 3 attempts: ...", which is logged as a genuine decision and scored by every metric — and given F-3, a spurious HOLD raises RG. (b) An exception inside the runner's try block skips the log row while the environment still advances, so days can vanish silently; the guard only trips below 10% completion. I checked the actual data: 4 fallback rows out of 966,700, and zero missing interior days. Neither has contaminated anything. Fix them as hygiene, not as remediation, and say so honestly if asked.

Two properties matter. Sampling base per run breaks the P/E = 15 fair-value anchor, which is what lets a memorised real-world prior score for free. The lag plus noise makes V statistically inferable but not algebraically recoverable — which is what you want, since a genuinely unlearnable ground truth would make the task impossible rather than hard. Then report the residual recoverability: fit the best predictor of V from Ot and publish its R², the way KTD-Fin publishes its de-anonymisation rate. That converts your weakest claim into a measured quantity.

09 — Validation

What would let us claim the generator is grounded rather than arbitrary

Under the reframing, “empirically grounded” must not mean “resembles a real market” — that would re-import the realism standard you have deliberately set aside, and it is a standard this environment will always lose against. It should mean: the instrument has known and reported measurement properties. That is the standard used for a physical instrument, it is achievable in six weeks, and it is a stronger claim than realism because it is checkable.

Ship this as a numbered subsection plus one appendix table. It is the section that answers all three reviewers at once.

10 — Scope

What to leave broken, and how to say so

Not fixing something is only defensible if the limitation is stated precisely enough that a reviewer cannot claim you were unaware of it. Below is what I would leave, with the argument for each. The pattern throughout: name the specific mechanism you are giving up, and say what it would and would not change. Vague limitations read as hedging; specific ones read as control.

Deliberate non-fixes and their scoping
Not fixingHow to scope it honestly
Multi-agent dynamics and an order book Drop the current argument, which is wrong. Say instead: endogenous price formation would make Vt a function of the agent's own trades, so the instrument would no longer have an exogenous reference. Acknowledge ABIDES explicitly — an exogenous fundamental oracle plus a limit order book is compatible with a latent ground truth — and say that this is the right architecture for studying market impact, which is not what we measure. That concedes the technical point while keeping the design decision.
Multiple assets and portfolio effects A single mandate parameter over a single risky asset is the minimal setting in which mandate adherence is identifiable. Cross-asset allocation would confound adherence with selection skill. Name that confound; it is the actual reason.
Transaction costs and slippage State it as a deliberate property: zero-cost trading makes over-trading behaviourally observable but economically free, which isolates compliance from profitability. Then state the price: no result here transfers to a cost-sensitive deployment, and returns reported in the paper are not comparable to any live benchmark. Cite CLQT as the cost-aware complement.
Realistic news and sentiment After B6 gives sentiment an AR(1) structure, the remaining gap is event-driven and fat-tailed dynamics. Keep the existing W6 argument — validity rests on decoupling, not sentiment realism — which becomes sound once V1 and V2 are shipped.
Psychometric validity of the personas The reframing kills this cleanly. Personas are instructions, not measured traits; MBTI is a source of instruction text and nothing else. Remove “psychometric” from the title, abstract and metric names. This answers nhWU's primary objection, Gxm9's second, and VG7b's W2 in one move, and it costs nothing. It is the highest-leverage sentence-level change in the whole plan.
Persona coverage beyond three profiles Unchanged from the current limitations text, which is adequate. Under the reframing the relevant axis is instruction difficulty, not trait coverage — reframe the limitation in those terms and it reads as scoped rather than thin.
Mandate wording sensitivity Keep as acknowledged-open, as we told VG7b. Note that the O3 numerical ablation gives a partial answer while being confounded (§03), and say so rather than over-claiming it a second time.
Real-market transfer State flatly and early that no result is claimed to transfer to live trading, and that the environment is a stimulus generator with a known latent state, not a market model. Saying this in the introduction rather than the limitations is what makes Gxm9's “no practical value” objection inapplicable rather than merely answered.
