# Review B: claims versus evidence (independent reviewer, 23 Aug 2026)

Reviewer lens: every claim in the deck (SPEAKER_SCRIPT.md, build_slides.py, the rendered slides), the E1/E6 documents, the generated audit files and the code that produced them. Five small checks were run in the reviewer's scratchpad: `check_bias.py` (ACF(1) estimator bias for the half-life), `check_analyst.py` (implemented analyst-error sd), `check_l1.py` (L1 error percentiles and omitted candidate formulas, 8-seed panel), `check_real_clustering.py` (the checklist's clustering/kurtosis criteria applied to real 200-day single-stock windows, 10 tickers 2000-2024 via yfinance), and `python -m evaluation.stylized_facts --env v2 --seeds 25 --engine ar1`.

Abbreviations: SS = SPEAKER_SCRIPT.md; BS = tools/build_slides.py; SPEC = E1_V2_GENERATOR_SPEC.md; CAL = CALIBRATION_REPORT.md; DL = DECISION_LOG.md; AMD = PREREGISTRATION_AMENDMENTS.md; CK1/CK2 = generated/checklist_v1.md / checklist_v2.md; LA1/LA2 = generated/leakage_audit_v1.md / _v2.md; SF = evaluation/stylized_facts.py; LAP = evaluation/leakage_audit.py.

## Headline

1. The generator's own numbers do not support the deck's central "hidden value" story. A fitted model on the shown fields recovers V to 3.5-4.9 % MAPE and x with R2 0.90-0.96 (LA2, 50-seed audit); the pre-registered L2 gate FAILS; slide 25 nevertheless says "the environment withholds the level of value" while CAL section 7 says the opposite ("the level of V is recoverable to ~4 % MAPE"). The "no formula reproduces V, >= 12 % off" headline is the median error of k*P, which is just the median |x|.
2. The "realised half-life ~72 d" is an estimator artefact. A pure AR(1) with true half-life 150 d gives a median sample half-life of 62 d on T=800 windows (simulation), 21 d on T=200, and only 54 % of paths clear the 60-day floor; the T=800/20-seed pass is a coin flip, and the same estimator gives 84-96 d for a process whose stationary half-life is ~580 d. The deck's "~70 days realised on a 200-day path" is wrong on both the number and the window.
3. "Nothing re-tuned to pass" is contradicted by the amendments log: A1, A2 and A6 changed the statistic or criterion of items 7, 9 and L1 after seeing fails, and each change flips a fail to a pass or margin; alpha/gamma/beta, sbar, jump rate, panic multiplier, phi and the hazard were all tuned against the checklist (eleven variants A-K).
4. Several numbers in the deliverables are wrong or not like-for-like: the sensitivity pass/fail counts on slide 25 and CAL section 5 disagree with their own tables in 5 of 6 variants; the analyst-error sd is implemented at 0.34, not the documented 0.15 (a sqrt(5) factor in `envs/v2/observables.py:130`); the "100 % -> 65 %" phase-from-calendar row compares v1 within-scenario with v2 mixed-set; the L2 "0.90 -> 0.83" row compares v1 full-feature with a stale v2 price-only number.
5. Under `engine=ar1` the checklist reproduces the FW result item for item (8/7, identical item-9 statistics). At the calibrated parameters (n_f = 0.997) the "published Franke-Westerhoff form" is an AR(1) in disguise; the slide's fundamentalist/chartist narrative is nominal.

## 3. Direct answers to the team lead's three concerns

(1) "return moments do not identify the persistence" rests on one stalled optimiser run and a 1-D profile at parameters where chartists never act; "150 d stationary / 70 d realised" is a 20-seed T=800 number reproduced by pure estimator bias and is 14 d on the benchmark's own 200-day windows; the event ranges are design choices with loose, partly unverified anchors.

(2) The hidden value is not unrecoverable. The team's own L2 and L5 audits recover V to ~4 % and x with R2 0.90-0.96 from what the agent sees; the 15 %/22 % median errors are the wrong statistic (medians of single linear formulas; p10 is 3-4 %) and partly a bug (analyst sd 0.34 instead of 0.15). The pre-registered L2 gate fails and the deck does not say so.

(3) 50 seeds x 200 days is adequate for share-type items (SE ~2 pp on 470 paths) but not for the persistence item (20 seeds, biased estimator), the L1/L2 audit (150 paths after subsampling), the L5 oracle (10 seeds) or the sensitivities (25 seeds). Longer horizons were used only for item 9 and only because the 200-day result failed (A2). The clustering item missed its 80 % target because the target is wrong for 200-day windows: real single stocks reject Ljung-Box at 1 % in only 29 % of 200-day windows, so the generator at 60 % is, if anything, more clustered than the data the criterion was meant to represent; items 2 and 3 need empirically re-derived thresholds, not a larger alpha.
